Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Short version: Bilye Tılsımı does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, gems, best scores), settings (aiming style, orientation, sound, music, vibration, language) and a flag that remembers whether you have seen the tutorial are saved locally on your device so the game can remember them. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this information.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • The game needs no internet connection and makes no network requests.

Children

The game contains no ads, no external links and no user-generated content. Since it collects no personal data, it is suitable for all ages.

Fonts and third-party material

The game uses the Cinzel and Manrope typefaces under the SIL Open Font License. Artwork and sound are generated in code for the game.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
