Frequently asked questions

How do I aim?

Open Settings and choose Dial (place a finger anywhere and turn it around the shooter, release to fire) or Direct Aim (the shooter points where you touch, release to fire). You can also change this while paused during a level. Tap the shooter to swap with the next marble.

How are gems earned?

Each level awards 1–3 gems depending on how close the chain came to the hole. The result screen explains exactly why you received the gems you did.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game never needs an internet connection.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on your device and is not backed up by the game itself. It follows your device backups made by Apple (iCloud or computer backups) when you restore to a new phone.

How do I change the language or reset progress?

Settings → Language. Settings → Reset progress.

The game shows the wrong layout or text is cut off.

Please email us your device model and a screenshot, we will fix it quickly.
