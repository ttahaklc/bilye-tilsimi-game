The chain is advancing and the talisman is in danger. Shoot the right marble into the right place, match the colors and stop the chain before it reaches the hole.

BİLYE TILSIMI is a calm but tense marble shooter set in a world of obsidian and gold. In every level the chain winds along a curved track while you aim from the centre and pop groups of three or more matching marbles. When the chain breaks, reactions cascade and combos multiply your score.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 100 levels across 10 worlds. Every world has its own backdrop, track shape, marble finish and shooter, from Dawn to Talisman.
• Endless mode with three maps: Spiral, Serpent and Rose. The chain speeds up as you score, your multiplier climbs to x7, and every map keeps its own record.
• Two ways to aim: Dial (turn your finger around the shooter, release to fire, your hand never covers the target) or Direct Aim (the shooter points where you touch). Switch any time, even mid-level.
• 1 to 3 gems per level, earned by how close the chain came to the hole. The result screen tells you exactly why.
• Tap the shooter to swap with the next marble for quick plays.
• Portrait or landscape, full screen on iPhone and iPad.
• Haptics, sound effects and music can each be turned off.

WHY BİLYE TILSIMI
• No ads. No purchases. No accounts. No tracking.
• Works fully offline: on a plane, on the road, anywhere.
• Türkçe, English, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, العربية and Français.
• Light and smooth: opens instantly, no loading screens, built for short sessions.
